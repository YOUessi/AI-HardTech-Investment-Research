04 / DECISION & RISK CONTROL

下一次委员会放行的必要条件

必须获取

  • Review latest Q3 operating cash flow and gross margin with documented customer mix and aftersales exposures
  • Reconcile IPO net proceeds and post-listing cash to construct enterprise value from same-date financials
  • Demand H1 to Q3 earnings continuity and evaluate whether 2025 share-based payment distorts normalization
  • Separate full-size humanoid vs quadruped contribution, verified sales to paying industrial users
  • Monitor unlock calendar and special-voting-share rights; avoid assuming all issued shares free float

核心反证

  • 2025 机器人出货及真实主营收入增长迅速，成本/渠道规模优势可能很强。
  • 但目前 2026H1 扣非利润下滑且流通盘较窄，大幅股价上涨不能证明 2027+ 经营表现。
  • 公告中的通用设备平均 P/E 不是同业务真实可比倍数，不能用其作买价。
  • 若拿不到 IPO 后一致口径净债务，拒绝伪造 EV 与年度自由现金流 DCF。
