Key model restrictions

Compute: linear within-year fleet ramp, idle electricity, depreciation on growth/maintenance capex, no NOL or financing-interest schedule, terminal EV/EBITDA heuristic. Robotics: average active installed base, no attrition, explicit hardware warranty and BOM, terminal EV/revenue heuristic. The models are not audited financial forecasts.
